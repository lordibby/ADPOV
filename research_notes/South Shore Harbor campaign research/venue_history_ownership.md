## What the property is and who owns/operates it

### Takeaway
South Shore Harbor is a public-land marina complex at 6701 Stars and Stripes Blvd, New Orleans 70126, next to Lakefront Airport. Land is state-created levee-district property managed by the Lakefront Management Authority (successor to the Orleans Parish Non-Flood Protection Asset Management Authority, NFPAMA); parcels are leased to private operators.

### Cited Findings
- Marina developed in the 1980s; roughly 447-453 open slips plus 26 covered (other listing says 479 total) - slip counts conflict across sources - [Edgewater Resources](https://www.edgewaterresources.com/project/south-shore-harbor/); [Lake-Pontchartrain.com](https://www.lake-pontchartrain.com/marina--south-shore-harbor-marina/2712); [Marinas.com](https://marinas.com/view/marina/16c5pvz_South_Shore_Harbor_Marina_New_Orleans_LA_United_States)
- The LMA manages South Shore Harbor and Orleans Marina (roughly six miles west); slip leases and applications run through marinasinneworleans.com, office at 6701 Stars and Stripes Blvd, marinainfo@nfpama.com - [Marinas in New Orleans](https://marinasinneworleans.com/leases/application)
- LMA statutory role: manages non-flood-protection assets within a levee district under a flood protection authority (i.e., Southeast Louisiana Flood Protection Authority-East, SLFPA-E) - [Murray Yacht Sales summary of LMA master plan](https://murrayyachtsales.com/marina-master-plan-report-lakefront-management-authority/)
- Former Bally's Casino site: 19 acres, owned by Orleans Levee District, managed by NFPAMA - [NOLA East](https://nolaeast.com/events/SOUTH%20SHORE%20HARBOR/south_shore_harbor_complex.htm)
- Edgewater Resources prepared a redevelopment study for both marinas, presentation dated May 30, 2024 - [Edgewater](https://www.edgewaterresources.com/project/south-shore-harbor/)
- Louisiana Act 83 (HB 654, 2024) requires lessee selection by RFP under a cooperative endeavor agreement for certain Orleans and Jefferson parcels - [legis.la.gov](https://www.legis.la.gov/Legis/ViewDocument.aspx?d=1358032) (relevance to this site not confirmed)
- Parcel "L" at the marina: LMA RFP/RFQ for a long-term ground lease, proposals due Apr 29, 2022, mandatory pre-proposal conference Mar 30, 2022; June 22, 2022 special meeting agenda had a motion to select a developer - [Public notice](https://www.centralauctionhouse.com/rfp25047351-request-for-proposalsqualifications-for-the-lease-of-parcel-l-located-at-south-shore-harbor-marina-new-orleans-la.html); [LMA June 22 2022 agenda](https://wwwcfprd.doa.louisiana.gov/boardsandcommissions/MeetingNotices/547_SPECIAL%20BOARD%20MEETING%20AGENDA%20-%20JUNE%2022%2022022.pdf)
- Restaurants/venues: South Shore Harbor Bar & Grill, 6901 Stars and Stripes Blvd, reported open (offers special events, private parties); Yelp page shows "updated October 2026" - [NOLA Lakefront](https://nolalakefront.com/news/south-shore-harbor-bar-grill-is-now-open/); [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/south-shore-harbor-bar-and-grill-new-orleans). Another site, sshdg.com ("South Shore Harbor"), surfaced and may be the venue's own site - [sshdg.com](https://www.sshdg.com/) (not fetched)

### Inferences
- Parcel "L" (marina side) and the 6901 Stars and Stripes (ex-Bally's) site are probably different parcels; the Coaxum Parcel L negotiation (2022-23) may or may not be the same lease as the Bally's-site lease described in 2024. Unresolved.

## Development history

### Takeaway
The ex-Bally's site was approved around 2015-16 for the Tipitina's/Roland von Kurnatowski "Lakeshore Landing" project (50-year lease, ~$12-13M); that lessee later lost control, and Coaxum Enterprises now holds the site.

### Cited Findings
- Nov 2015: von Kurnatowski (Tipitina's owner) planned a ~$12M "Lakefront Festival Park" on the site - [Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2015/11/27/tipitinas-owner-plans-12m-lakefront-festival-park/); [Biz New Orleans](https://bizneworleans.com/tipitinas-owner-plans-12m-lakefront-festival-park/)
- City Council gave unanimous final approval to a 5,000-person live-entertainment venue (outdoor amphitheater, fuel dock, marine store) on the 19-acre site under a 50-year lease; cost est. $13M - [NOLA East](https://nolaeast.com/events/SOUTH%20SHORE%20HARBOR/south_shore_harbor_complex.htm); [Advocate](https://www.theadvocate.com/new_orleans/news/developers-propose-outdoor-music-venue-and-water-park-on-n-o-lakefront/article_85049688-56f1-5232-90dc-3fbca06db699.amp.html)
- Landing Festival (Galactic + Tipitina's) was planned for Sept 26-27 (2015), then postponed because of amphitheater construction - [OffBeat](https://offbeat.com/news/landing-festival-postponed-due-lakefront-amphitheater-project)
- Lakeshore Landing held free "Hard Hat" concerts during construction; Lazy Jack bar opened there; 2017 report: marina convenience store/fuel dock next - [OffBeat](https://www.offbeat.com/news/lakeshore-landing-hard-hat-concerts/); [OffBeat](https://offbeat.com/news/lazy-jack-opens-lakeshore-landing-serving-rock)
- OffBeat reported the von Kurnatowskis "lost ownership" of Tipitina's over alleged fraud by Roland von Kurnatowski; this is an allegation, with no date or case outcome found - [OffBeat](https://www.offbeat.com/news/tiptinas-owner-agrees-develop-new-orleans-lakefront-property) (single source, snippet). Do not repeat in pitch.
- 2022: LMA Parcel L RFP; Aug 2022 negotiation authority for Coaxum Enterprises; Jan 2023 rescission motion (see above).

### Gaps
- Katrina and Ida damage/rebuild details not found. Hotel plan not found. When the von Kurnatowski lease ended and Coaxum took over is not documented in what I could reach. Controversies beyond the above not found.
