### Takeaway

## 3. Weddings, luxury events and private aviation

### Takeaway
No authoritative New Orleans-specific wedding average found; national average about $36K (Zola 2025), with New Orleans estimates $35K-$60K from non-survey calculators. Lakefront Airport sees huge event spikes (about 1,200 jets expected for Super Bowl LIX vs about 200 daily movements).

### Cited Findings
- Zola projection: national average 2025 wedding $36,000, up from $33,000 in 2024 — [Axios New Orleans](https://www.axios.com/local/new-orleans/2025/02/14/2025-wedding-trends-louisiana-prices-gifts)
- Local outlet says The Knot didn't list New Orleans; Louisiana near national average, a bit above Southern states — [My New Orleans](https://www.myneworleans.com/how-much-does-a-wedding-cost-in-louisiana/)
- Calculator estimates (not survey data): $35K-$60K New Orleans; Wedding.report $39.6K-$53.4K for 175 guests — [WeddingBot](https://weddingbot-ai.pages.dev/cost/new-orleans-la-wedding-cost/), [Wedding.report](https://wedding.report/action/wedding_cost.estimate/budgetid/A766CFC5-9928-0412-C12D35A2BFC6C107)
- Super Bowl LIX (Feb 2025): about 1,200 private aircraft projected at Lakefront, more than triple usual traffic; normal day about 200 operations, expected 400 by Super Bowl Sunday; airport used much of its 473 acres for parking; MSY as overflow — [Fox 8](https://www.fox8live.com/2025/01/31/lakefront-airport-gears-up-super-bowl-lixs-vip-influx/), [AviationPros](https://www.aviationpros.com/airports/news/55262747/super-bowl-lix-will-bring-an-unprecedented-stream-of-vip-private-jets-to-lakefront-airport), [Flexjet](https://flexjet.com/en-us/blog/from-takeoff-to-kickoff-flexjet-prepares-for-seamless-private-jet-travel-to-superbowl-lix)
- Actual regional count: 611 business jet arrivals Fri-Sun across New Orleans-area airports (not Lakefront alone) — [GlobalAir](https://www.globalair.com/articles/new-orleans-sees-a-super-bowl-shuffle-of-jets-as-2025-starts-strong-for-bizav/8566)
- FBO special event fees applied, like Las Vegas — [AIN](https://www.ainonline.com/aviation-news/business-aviation/2025-02-07/special-event-fees-tap-super-bowl-new-orleans)
- 2013 Super Bowl: about 700 jets expected at Lakefront; near its approximately 600-aircraft limit — [AIN](https://backend.ainonline.com/aviation-news/business-aviation/2013-03-03/bizjets-flock-big-easy-nfls-big-game)

### Inferences
- Event-driven private-jet traffic (Super Bowl, CFP title game Jan 2028, Essence, Sugar Bowl) is a plausible tie-in for luxury hospitality/fly-in weddings, but no data links jet traffic to wedding demand.

### Gaps
- No celebrity/destination wedding data for New Orleans; no Lakefront annual operations or Jazz Fest/Mardi Gras jet data; no verified Lakefront-only Super Bowl LIX actuals.

## 6. Marina/waterfront revitalization case studies

### Takeaway
Quantified outcomes are thin; Baltimore Inner Harbor is the best-documented but is a very different scale.

### Cited Findings
- Baltimore Inner Harbor IMPLAN study: $2.3B economic activity, 21,000 jobs, $102M annual tax revenue — [IMPLAN case study](https://www.casestudies.com/company/implan/case-study/harboring-tourism-reinvesting-in-baltimores-inner-harbor)
- Newcastle City Marina claims it paid for itself three times over on visitor spend (developer claim, no method) — [Newcastle NE1](https://www.newcastlene1ltd.com/projects/newcastle-city-marina)
- Waukegan Harbor: rising attendance, many first-time visitors (figures not seen) — [GLC](https://www.glc.org/wp-content/uploads/Waukegan-092418.pdf)
- A Virginia county marina saw attendance grow FY2017-2019 while revenue dropped — [WY Daily](https://wydaily.com/latest/local/2019/09/23/marina-attendance-grows-but-revenue-drops-heres-what-the-county-plans-to-do-about-it/)

### Inferences
- Lesson from the Virginia example: attendance growth must be paired with monetization (ticketing, F&B, rentals).

### Gaps
- No comparable small-marina campaign with measurable before/after (e.g., Chicago Northerly Island, Pittsburgh, Tampa) was retrieved.
