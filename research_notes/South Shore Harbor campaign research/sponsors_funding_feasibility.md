# South Shore Harbor (New Orleans) - Sponsorship Landscape and Funding Feasibility

Research date: November 2026. Method note: WebFetch was blocked for most hosts (bankingdive.com, bizneworleans.com, ir.lamar.com, canalstreetbeat.com did not resolve in this session), so all findings below come from WebSearch result summaries of the cited URLs, not full-page reads. Treat figures as needing primary-source confirmation where flagged.

## 1. Is "Nola Bank" a real institution? (proposed title sponsor)

### Takeaway
Nola Bank is real, but it is a brand-new de novo community bank that opened in September 2026 with about $29.2M in capital. It has no sponsorship track record and likely a small marketing budget, so a six-figure title sponsorship is a stretch. Nothing needs to be re-named.

### Cited Findings
- Nola Bank opened in New Orleans as Louisiana's first new bank since 2010 (first new bank chartered in the state in 16 years) - [NewOrleans.com / New Orleans & Company](https://www.neworleans.com/articles/post/nola-bank-opens-in-new-orleans-as-louisianas-first-new-bank-since-2010/); [Biz New Orleans](https://bizneworleans.com/nola-bank-opens-as-states-1st-in-16-years/)
- Received final regulatory approval Sept. 11 and opened Sept. 14, per CEO Joe Omner. Banking Dive calls it the eighth de novo to open "this year" - [Banking Dive](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/new-orleans-de-novo-nola-bank-community-mdi-fdic/830889/). (The year is not stated in the snippet; "first since 2010 / 16 years" implies 2026.)
- Raised $29.2M in capital from investors - [Banking Dive](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/new-orleans-de-novo-nola-bank-community-mdi-fdic/830889/); [Biz New Orleans](https://bizneworleans.com/nola-bank-opens-as-states-1st-in-16-years/)
- HQ: 1100 S. Carrollton Ave., New Orleans; operating from a temporary banking facility while a permanent building is built on the same site; no branches listed - [NewOrleans.com](https://www.neworleans.com/articles/post/nola-bank-opens-in-new-orleans-as-louisianas-first-new-bank-since-2010/)
- Leadership: Joe Omner, President and CEO; Henry L. Coaxum, Jr., Chairman - same press coverage as above.
- Stated mission: serve the underbanked and become a preferred small-business lender in the New Orleans area (Banking Dive URL suggests an MDI / community-development orientation; not confirmed in snippet) - [Banking Dive](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/new-orleans-de-novo-nola-bank-community-mdi-fdic/830889/)
- Do not confuse with unrelated institutions: Bank of New Orleans, Heritage NOLA Bancorp - [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_of_New_Orleans)
- A web search for Nola Bank/Omner sponsoring any community event in 2026 returned nothing - [search result set](https://www.neworleans.com/fit-fete/community-games/) (no match).

### Inferences
- With ~$29.2M capital (not assets), a new bank would typically spend modestly on marketing; $100K+ cash from one event is likely a meaningful share of its annual marketing budget. Plausible for a small-business/community-oriented launch pitch, but unlikely as a first-year title commitment without a strong local-impact story. (Inference, no source on its marketing budget.)
- Its newness is a pro (needs local brand awareness) and a con (no history, regulatory focus on safety/soundness).

### Gaps
- No marketing leader or sponsorship contact identified; total assets/deposits unverified (visbanking page shows $0, a stale new-filer artifact); FDIC BankFind not accessed.
- Whether it is an MDI is unconfirmed.

"Jay Johnson" is Lamar's CFO (Jay L. Johnson), not a local marketing or New Orleans contact.

## 4. Sponsorship benchmarks

### Takeaway
Public data does not give a clean average for mid-size festivals. The best hard data: alcohol leads festival sponsorship, and total rights fees across 39 major U.S. music festivals were $169M+ in 2025. A $100K per-sponsor deal is credible only with proven audience, which a first-year/relaunch event lacks.

### Cited Findings
- IEG 2025 Music Festivals report: 39 U.S. festivals, $169M+ in rights fees (Jan-Oct 2025), excluding activation and media; alcoholic beverages lead; pop festivals $103M+, rock $18M+ - [IEG](https://sponsorship.com/2025/11/18/2025-music-festivals/); [IEG PDF](https://sponsorship.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Music-Festivals_2025-Sector-Report_LI-Carousel_Posted-11.14.2025.pdf)
- Industry commentary: increased competition for festival sponsors in 2026, sponsors want measurable outcomes, bespoke rather than tiered packages, and year-one festivals do better with fewer, deeper partners - [FestivalPro](https://festivalpro.com/festival-management/4616/news/2026/1/14/Strategies-for-Handling-Increased-Competition-for-Music-Festival-Sponsors.html); [FestivalPro](https://festivalpro.com/festival-management/4560/news/2025/12/15/What-Sponsors-Want-from-Music-Festival-Deals-in-Changing-Times.html); [Ticket Fairy](https://branded.ticketfairy.com/blog/fewer-sponsors-deeper-partnerships-sponsorship-strategies-for-year-one-festivals)

### Inferences
- $169M/39 is about $4.3M per festival (my arithmetic; dominated by big festivals), so it overstates mid-size economics.
- Sponsorship typically covers a share (commonly cited rule of thumb, unsourced here) of costs; a plan relying on one or two $100K commitments against a $1M budget implies 10 or more sponsors, so sponsor math is aggressive. Sales cycles of 6-12 months are standard industry lore but I found no sourced figure; flag as unverified.
- Sponsors typically require audience demographics, attendance history, deliverables, activation rights, exclusivity, and post-event reporting (consistent with the commentary above, not quantified).

### Gaps
- No sourced average fee for mid-size festival or venue relaunch events; no sourced acquisition-cycle length; IEG full report is paywalled.

## 5. Public funding and incentives

### Takeaway
Public money is small relative to the plan: state tourism marketing grants have historically been about $10K, and the film credit targets qualifying productions, not event branded content (not confirmed either way).

### Cited Findings
- Louisiana Office of Tourism Competitive Grant Program (marketing for events): apply 90+ days before event, one award per event per year, three-year limit, reimbursable after event; historically up to $10,000 (FY20; current cap unverified) - [CGP FY25 guide](https://www.explorelouisiana.com/sites/default/files/2024-11/CGP%20FY25%20Application%20Guide%20FINAL%20with%20Video.docx); [Biz New Orleans](https://bizneworleans.com/la-office-of-tourism-announces-two-grant-programs/)
- Event Incentive Fund (Act 751 of 2022): application 180 days before the event - [Major Events application](https://www.explorelouisiana.com/sites/default/files/2023-09/Major%20Events%20Application_20230925.pdf) (amounts not found)
- Music Ambassador program: $50,000 total for FY2027, max $2,000 per talent per fiscal year - [Explore Louisiana](https://www.explorelouisiana.com/articles/louisiana-music-ambassadors)
- Film credit: Act 44 (signed June 4, 2025) reportedly raised the credit from 30% to 40% for approved projects and moved administration into LED; sources conflict on cap ($150M vs $125M) - [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/unitedstates/tax-authorities/1636658/governor-landry-signs-act-44-to-modify-the-film-production-tax-credit-program); [Vitrina](https://vitrina.ai/blog/louisiana-film-tax-credit); [KALB](https://www.kalb.com/2024/11/19/louisiana-legislature-backs-off-keeps-film-tax-credit-while-reducing-cap/)

### Inferences
- Event-promotional content may not qualify; a legal/tax review of "branded content" eligibility is needed before counting any credit.

### Gaps
- New Orleans & Company grant/funding programs not found; whether commercials/branded content qualify is unresolved.

## 6. Commercial video production rates (to check the $5,000 proposal)

### Takeaway
$5,000 buys roughly a one-day small-crew shoot with a short edited deliverable (e.g., a 30-90 second spot or a few social cuts). It does not cover a multi-day campaign, talent, drone permits, music licensing, or paid media.

### Cited Findings
- Corporate video commonly quoted at $1,000-$10,000 per finished minute; social short-form $500-$3,000/min; brand films $8,000-$25,000/min - [Vidico](https://vidico.com/news/corporate-video-cost/); [DesignRush](https://www.designrush.com/agency/video-marketing/trends/cost-of-video-production)
- Crew day rates: director $800-$2,500, DP $600-$2,000; small-to-mid crew $800-$4,000/day, 3-5 person team $2,500-$8,000/day - [Levitate Media](https://levitatemedia.com/learn/video-production-costs-explained-what-you-need-to-know); [Viva Media](https://vivamedia.ca/blog/corporate-video-pricing)
- One survey puts the average 60-second commercial corporate video at $3,213 (range $1,000-$16,000) - [Advids](https://advids.co/pricing/how-much-commercial-corporate-video-creation-cost)

### Inferences
- Sources are vendors with stake; no Louisiana-specific rate source found. $5,000 is plausible as a lean starter package, and Louisiana pricing is likely within these national ranges (unverified).

### Gaps
- No Louisiana-specific or IATSE/union rate data.

## 7. Legal and contract flags

### Takeaway
Events on public-land leases need landlord authority approval plus a stack of city and state permits; confirm who actually controls South Shore Harbor and what the lease permits.

### Cited Findings
- Site governance: South Shore Harbor is on former Levee District land managed by the Lakefront Management Authority (formerly the Non-Flood Protection Asset Management Authority); LMA adopted a Marina Master Plan for Orleans Marina and South Shore Harbor in Oct 2024; a notice sought ground-lease proposals for Parcel L, and a 2022 agenda includes a motion to select a developer - [LMA Oct 2024 agenda](https://wwwcfprd.doa.louisiana.gov/boardsAndCommissions/MeetingNotices/547_OCT%2024%202024%20-Regular%20Board%20Meeting%20Agenda__final10222024.pdf); [Public notice](https://www.centralauctionhouse.com/rfp25047351-request-for-proposalsqualifications-for-the-lease-of-parcel-l-located-at-south-shore-harbor-marina-new-orleans-la.html); [2022 special meeting agenda](https://wwwcfprd.doa.louisiana.gov/boardsandcommissions/MeetingNotices/547_SPECIAL%20BOARD%20MEETING%20AGENDA%20-%20JUNE%2022%202022.pdf)
- City permitting: each activity needs its own permit (tents, parades, etc.); alcohol needs a temporary city ABO plus state ATC permit; Revenue Bureau lists occupational license, mayoralty permit, alcohol permit and performance bond or sales tax deposit - [City Special Event Guide](https://www.nola.gov/nola/media/One-Stop-Shop/Office%20of%20Cultural%20Economy/Special-Event-Guide.pdf); [BOR form](https://nola.gov/nola/media/One-Stop-Shop/Revenue/BOR-Special-Event-Permit-ABO.pdf). The guide carries a notice that older intake channels are closed; confirm current process via One Stop Shop or specialevents@nola.gov.

### Inferences
- Sponsor-funded events likely need the lessee/landlord consent, additional-insured coverage, liquor liability and sponsor exclusivity cleared against existing lease and any alcohol vendor. New Orleans insurance minimums were not found.

### Gaps
- Current lessee, executed lease terms, and whether any entity can host events or sell sponsorship rights at South Shore Harbor are unconfirmed (no 2025-2026 news found). New Orleans insurance limits not found.
